# History
The world initially contained one single deity that was responsible for the creation of this universe. Then, due to some event, the god was split into 7 different pieces; the Concepts of Creation.
These concepts are all responsible for different aspects of the universe, briefly explained in [[Concepts of Creation]]
- Change
	- Representing the everchanging nature of the world and everything in it
	- Captured as an individual with a fixed, persistent identity
- Connection
	- Representing what keeps the world together
	- Captured as a spell on a floating island protected by mecahnical beings
- Exploration
	- Representing the desire for variation and knowledge that all beings in the world posess
	- Captured as a creature that follows a predefined and specific path
- Flow
	- Representing the driving force behind the developments in the world
	- Captured as a spherical artifact 
- Growth
	- Representing the world developing itself
	- Captured as an evergreen tree of life that does not grow
- Reflection
	- Representing the introspection and how all in the world has an opposite
	- Captured as a dark and obscure cave, surrounded by a perfectly still lake.
- Whimsy
	- Representing the absurdity of the world, and how not all of it makes sense
	- Captured as a well-defined dimension with hard rules

As they are all aspects of the God of Creation, they can all be combined to reshape the world and effectively amass the same power that the GOd of Creation once had

Throughout the development of civilisation, the concepts have not been of constant form, and have spread across the location around which the God got fractured - this being the Makhshar desert, which whas desertified due to this event and remnants of this can still be found on the surface
